## 👋 About me

<img src="ChrisNo86/tree/main/assets" width="320" align="right" alt="Christian Noack at his desk in front of the CN Security & Systems logo"/>

I'm Christian, and I run **CN Security & Systems** in Detmold, Germany.

I'm a trained **IT specialist for systems integration** and **electrician**, and I build for the web. That combination is what I offer: I get IT, networks and smart homes running for private customers and small businesses, and I design websites by hand, with security built in from the first line.

* 🖥️ PC help on site around Detmold and by remote support
* 🏠 Smart home and IT installation, from cabling to the app
* 🎨 Handmade websites and WordPress designs
* 🛡️ Security advice that people without an IT department can follow
